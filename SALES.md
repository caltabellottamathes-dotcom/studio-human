# STUDIO HUMAN — Template Tiers (Sales Spec)

A contemporary psychology-practice platform, sold as four cumulative tiers from
a single codebase. Each tier is a complete, usable product on its own.

## Tier 1 — Marketing + Self-Reflection
A polished public website for a contemporary psychology practice, plus an
interactive self-reflection questionnaire that guides visitors toward a relevant
concern and a gentle invitation to reach out.

- Public marketing site (Home, Approach, Concerns, About, Pricing, Contact)
- Self-reflection lightbox with weighted profile results
- Contact request capture
- Minimalist "Grey Sky" aesthetic, fully responsive
- **Best for:** Practices that only need an online presence and lead capture.
- **License key:** Not required.

## Tier 2 — Practice Management (Admin Dashboard)
Everything in Tier 1, plus a complete admin dashboard for running the practice
digitally — used internally by the practice, with no client self-service yet.

- Admin login
- Client management, scheduling, session notes, assignments, secure messaging, documents, invoices, settings, assessment editor
- **Best for:** Practices that want to digitize their practice management before
  offering clients a self-service portal.
- **Note:** At this tier the admin can create appointments, share documents, and
  send messages, but with no client portal those records are internal until
  upgraded to Tier 3.
- **License key:** Not required.

## Tier 3 — Full Platform (Admin + Client Portal)
Everything in Tier 2, plus a private, authenticated client portal so clients can
engage between sessions — fed and managed from the admin dashboard.

- Client login
- Dashboard, appointments, secure messaging, documents, assignments, mood tracking, invoices, profile
- The admin dashboard from Tier 2 manages everything the portal surfaces
- **Best for:** Practices ready to give clients self-service on top of full
  practice management.
- **License key:** Not required.

## Tier 4a — Full Platform + Resale License
Everything in Tier 3, plus the right to resell or white-label the template to
other practices.

- Centralized brand configuration (name, tagline, contact, colors)
- License-gated resale layer with HMAC key validation
- One license key per buyer instance, bound to their workspace
- **Best for:** Agencies and consultants serving multiple practices.
- **License key:** Required (issued per sale).

## What's included in every sale
- A provisioned Base44 app instance set to the purchased tier
- Rebranding to the buyer's practice name and contact details
- Deployment to the buyer's domain
- 14 days of post-delivery bug-fix support

## Optional add-ons
- **Rebranding service** — custom copy, fonts, imagery, color tuning
- **Extended support** — monthly maintenance retainer
- **Additional license keys** (Tier 4a only) — per extra instance

## Delivery timeline
- Tier 1–3: 3–5 business days from payment
- Tier 4a: 5–7 business days (includes license issuance)

## Why the tiers are ordered this way
The client portal depends on the admin dashboard that feeds it — appointments,
documents, messages, and assignments all originate on the admin side. So the
dashboard ships at Tier 2 (practice management) and the portal ships at Tier 3
on top of it. A portal without a dashboard would be an empty shell with no one
to populate or respond to it.
